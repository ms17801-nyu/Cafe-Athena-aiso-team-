Why does Greek and Mediterranean food suit these diets?

Traditional Greek cooking is built around vegetables, legumes, olive oil, herbs, lemon and grilled meats or fish. Many dishes in this style are vegetarian or vegan by nature. Many are also gluten-free because they rely on vegetables, beans, rice and grilled proteins rather than wheat. Wheat usually appears in specific items such as pita and pastry, so a diner can often pick around it.
